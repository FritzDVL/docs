# Governance & Core Badges

Governance and Core Badges represent operational responsibility, voting weight in the meritocratic DAO, and strategic oversight within Society Protocol.

---

## DAO Governance Power Split

The SP DAO governance power is distributed meritocratically between execution and strategic guidance:

- **Core Team (~38% Voting Power):** Core Team Badges (`25`) grant execution leadership to full-time researchers, engineers, and operational stewards.
- **Affiliates & Partners (~62% Voting Power):** Advisor Badges (`24`) and Partner Community Badges grant strategic oversight to accredited advisors and partner communities.

---

## Badge Roles & Responsibilities

### DAO Badge (ID: 11)
Issued to official SP DAO community members. Grants forum access and proposal feedback rights.

### Security Council Badge (ID: 12)
Awarded to elected members of the Security Council. Grants emergency multisig keyholder privileges and circuit-breaker controls.

### Governor Badge (ID: 13)
Awarded to meritocratically appointed Governors who execute executive DAO voting power and protocol stewardship.

### Advisor Badge (ID: 24)
Awarded to strategic advisors, industry experts, and accredited partner institutions providing long-term guidance.

### Core Team Badge (ID: 25)
Awarded to core protocol researchers, developers, and operators following 6 months of continuous full-time contribution.

### Contributor / Builder Badge (ID: 26)
Awarded to open-source developers, researchers, and community builders who merge research, code, or architecture contributions.

### Moderator Badge (ID: 28)
Issued to community operators and moderators managing Web3 Outpost forums and official communication channels.
